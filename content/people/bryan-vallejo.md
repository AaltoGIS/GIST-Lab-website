Bryan is a doctoral researcher at GIST Lab (May 2025 onwards). He is working with HPC (High Performance Computers) from CSC focused in improving *Geospatial Analysis* with *Parallel Computing.*

Bryan first joined the GIST Lab – Aalto University as a *Technical Specialist* on 2023. He is part of the *Geoportti Project* and supports the *[Geospatial Challenge Camp](https://challenge-camp.geoportti.fi/en/latest/index.html)* in cooperation with Finnish partners like the *University of Turku*, and other privates companies participating in the course.

He has experience working in spatial analysis algorithms in urban areas focused on mobility and accessibility, specially in Finland and Estonia. He is fan of writing educational material for the geospatial community using Python programming in his personal *[Medium Blog](https://medium.com/@bryanvallejo16)*. The highlight of his work is dedicated to wildlife conservation focused on movement analysis and Earth Observation next to *[GIS4 Wildlife](https://www.gis4-wildlife.com/)*.
