Eero Alkio finished and submitted his MSc thesis in July called “*Spatially distributed crop modelling in the Mekong River Basin with AquaCrop*“. Congrats Eero! This work emerged as a result from fruitful collaboration between Geoinformatics + Water and Environmental Engineering research groups.

Eero’s thesis relates to the postdoctoral research of Josias Láng-Ritter who was also Eero’s thesis advisor together with Dr. Matias Heino. Henrikki was the supervisor of Eero.
