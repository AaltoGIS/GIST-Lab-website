Ngoc Chau finished and submitted her MSc thesis in July called “A geospatial data-driven approach to assess transport-related carbon emissions: Case Otaniemi, Finland“. Congrats Ngoc! Ngoc did her work as a research assistant at GIST Lab together with Henrikki who was her main supervisor and advisor. Also Prof. Milos Mladenović from the Spatial Planning and Transport Engineering group gave valuable guidance for the thesis.

You can read Ngoc’s excellent work from here: [https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/handle/123456789/122860](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/handle/123456789/122860)
